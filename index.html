Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times? +
Check-in time is 8:00 AM and check-out time is 7:00 AM. Early check-in and late check-out are subject to availability — please contact us in advance via WhatsApp or phone.
How far is Puri Beach from the hotel? +
Puri Beach (Golden Beach) is just 200 metres from the hotel — a comfortable 2-minute walk. The hotel is directly opposite Swargadwar Sea Beach on New Marine Drive Road.
How far is Shree Jagannath Temple? +
Shree Jagannath Temple is approximately 1.6 km from the hotel — a 5–8 minute auto-rickshaw ride or a 20-minute walk. This makes us the ideal hotel for pilgrims visiting the temple.
Is parking available at the hotel? +
Yes, free parking is available for guests travelling by private vehicle. Please inform us at the time of booking if you require parking.
Do you have a restaurant and room service? +
Yes! Our in-house restaurant serves authentic Odia cuisine, North Indian dishes, and more. Room service is available on request — simply call the front desk.
Can families book interconnected rooms? +
Yes, we offer Family Suites accommodating up to 4 adults, as well as interconnected room options for larger groups and extended families. Please contact us via WhatsApp for specific requirements.
What safety & security measures are in place? +
The hotel has 24/7 CCTV coverage inside and outside the premises, 24-hour front desk, fire extinguishers, smoke detectors, first-aid kits, and security guards on duty.
Is WiFi available? What is the ID proof requirement? +
Free WiFi is available throughout the property. As per government regulations, all guests above 18 years must carry a valid photo identity card and address proof at check-in.
